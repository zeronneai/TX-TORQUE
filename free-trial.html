Try Torque

Your first class is free

Your athlete's first class at TX Torque is free, with no deposit and no commitment. Trial classes run Fridays and Saturdays, year-round, for ages 7 and up and all skill levels.

Three steps to day one

  1. Book a free trial. Pick a Friday or Saturday slot. Your first class is free, no deposit.
  2. Train with the staff. Your athlete trains with our coaches in the right age group.
  3. Choose your plan. Join with the membership that fits your schedule and goals.

Prefer to talk first? Message us on WhatsApp at +1 915-474-0940 and a coach will get back to you.

Questions

Is the first class really free?

Yes. Your athlete's first trial class is free with no deposit. Trials run Fridays and Saturdays, year-round, for all skill levels.

When are trial classes?

Fridays at 4:00, 5:00 or 6:00 PM and Saturdays at 12:00, 1:00 or 2:00 PM. We confirm your exact time within 24 hours.

What should my athlete bring?

Athletic clothes, turf shoes or sneakers, a glove, a bat if they have one and a water bottle. We take care of the rest.

What ages can try it?

Ages 7 and up, all skill levels.
