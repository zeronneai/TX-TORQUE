How we train hitters

Hitting at Torque is part of one connected system. What we build in the weight room shows up in the cage, and the numbers prove it.

  • Mechanics: swing work taught by coaches who hit at the professional level in the U.S. and Mexico.
  • Power: rotational power and speed work that turns strength into bat speed.
  • Data: TrackMan tracks exit velocity (how hard the ball comes off the bat) and launch angle (the flight that turns contact into damage) on every swing.
  • Reps: five batting cages with machines on every lane at our new facility.
