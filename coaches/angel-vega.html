Angel Vega

Angel Vega caught in college at St. Mary's and San Jacinto, earned ABCA/Rawlings All-Region First Team honors, and caught professionally in the Mexican League with Algodoneros and Pericos de Puebla. He leads Torque's specialized catching coaching, one session per week, and also works with hitters.
