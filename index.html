Is the first class really free?

Yes. Your athlete's first trial class is free with no deposit. Trials run Fridays and Saturdays, year-round, for all skill levels.

What ages do you train?

Ages 7 and up, in three groups: 7 to 10, 11 to 14 and 14+. Each group trains at its own time so athletes work with peers at their level.

What should my athlete bring?

Athletic clothes, turf shoes or sneakers, a glove, a bat if they have one and a water bottle. We take care of the rest.

How do memberships work?

Choose a level by lessons per month (A, AA, AAA or MLB unlimited) and pay monthly, or save 10%, 15% or 20% with 6-month, 12-month or annual plans. Everything is managed in the Torque app.

Do you help with recruiting?

Yes. Our staff has real college and pro connections, runs exposure events and guides families through the recruiting process. TrackMan numbers give coaches the data they ask for.

Where are you located?

Starting Monday, November 2 we train at our new facility: 2010 Bassett Ave, El Paso, TX 79901.
