Our Story

This Is Torque

Torque was built by players who lived the professional game — and came home to raise the standard. This is our story: where we come from, what we believe, and why the next generation of El Paso baseball is being developed the right way.

The Torque Advantage

Why Train Here

MLB & Pro Experience

Coached by former MLB and professional players — draft picks, Hall of Famers, and pros who bring the real standards of the game to every session.

TrackMan Technology

Every rep is measured. Velocity, spin rate, exit velo, launch angle — TrackMan data drives development the same way it does in the big leagues.

Recruiting & Connections

We open doors. Real college and pro connections, exposure events, and guidance to get your athlete seen, recruited, and to the next level.

Structured Development

A professional development system — mechanics, strength & conditioning, and mindset — built in stages. No random reps, only deliberate progress.

Elite Facility

A performance facility built to pro standards — cages, mounds, and training tech — with year-round programming and weekend trial access.

The Complete Athlete

We build discipline, leadership, and mental toughness on and off the field — because the game rewards the athletes who master both.
