Training

Pitching lessons in El Paso

The TX Torque pitching program is led by Justin Maese, a 3rd round pick in the 2015 MLB Draft with nine years of pro experience. Pitchers train on a dedicated pitching lane with a mound, and TrackMan measures velocity and spin rate on every pitch.

Book a Free TrialWhatsApp +1 915-474-0940

How we train pitchers

  • Mechanics: delivery work from coaches who pitched in the Yankees, Braves and Blue Jays organizations and in the Mexican League.
  • Strength and power: age-appropriate strength and conditioning plus rotational power work that turns into velocity. See strength and conditioning.
  • Data: TrackMan measures pitch velocity in MPH and spin rate, the movement and quality of every pitch.
  • Space: a dedicated pitching lane with a mound at our new facility.

Want to understand the process first? Read our guides on how to increase pitching velocity and what age to start pitching lessons.
